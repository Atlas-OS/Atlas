# Atlas reports

Send an issue or suggestion directly from Atlas Manager or
[the report website](https://reports.atlasos.net). Describe what happened and
include diagnostics if useful. Contact details are optional.

Reports go privately to the Atlas team. Nothing is uploaded automatically:
you choose what to include and confirm before sending. When Atlas Manager
exports diagnostics, it removes your user name, PC name, email addresses and
known passwords or keys, and keeps the technical details needed to investigate,
such as error details, hardware models and app names. You're welcome to check
the ZIP first. Messages and contact details are sent as written.

Reports are deleted after 90 days, and copies the team downloads are deleted
within the same 90 days. The team can delete reports sooner through the private
dashboard. Access requires an authorized account and two-factor login, or a
scoped access key the team issues to an investigation tool; reports and
attachments are never publicly listed. The team may use AI services from other
companies to help investigate: these get a report's message and diagnostics,
never its contact details. Hosting security services may record connection
details, including IP addresses.

You can still export diagnostics and report through Discord if preferred.

This folder contains the public Rust code that validates, stores and manages
reports. See [service details](API.md) for how it works and
[deployment documentation](deploy/README.md) for maintenance.
